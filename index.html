What TipuCek can check

One checker for every way a scam can reach you.

  • Text messages — SMS, WhatsApp, DM or email, any pasted text
  • Screenshots — chat images read with OCR
  • Links & sites — shorteners, fake domains, brand look-alikes
  • Link tracking — follow a link to its real destination, registrar and registration date
  • Phone numbers — Indonesian numbers, with a report link
  • Bank accounts — account numbers, with the official checker
  • Files — APK, programs, archives and documents, by name or upload
  • Invitations — fake jobs, fake prizes, fake invitations

Not checked: QR-code images and payment codes — TipuCek does not scan those. A voice call can be checked: record it or upload a recording, and TipuCek transcribes it.

        How it works

        1

        Paste

        Drop in any suspicious message — SMS, WhatsApp, DM or email. No sign-up, no personal data.

        2

        Check

        A Gemini model on your device answers first. If it cannot, the cloud model (gemini-3.5-flash-lite) steps in — usually within seconds.

        3

        Decide

        Get a verdict, a risk score out of 100, the red flags found, and exactly what to do next.

        TipuCek gives a risk assessment, not a guarantee. When in doubt, verify the sender through an official channel and never share OTPs, PINs or passwords.

        On-device AI needs a supported browser (Chrome with Gemini Nano). On other browsers the cloud model answers instead — the result always says which one.
