    How it works

    1. On-device first. With Firebase AI Logic hybrid inference (PREFER_ON_DEVICE), the message is analyzed by a model running on this device — it never leaves your phone or computer.
    2. Cloud fallback. If the on-device model cannot answer, the request goes to Gemini in the cloud (gemini-3.5-flash-lite). The result card always shows which one answered: ON_DEVICE or IN_CLOUD.
    3. Private history. Results are saved to Firestore under an anonymous account. Without Firebase configured, history stays in this browser's local storage only.

    PesanAman gives a risk assessment, not a guarantee. When in doubt, verify the sender through an official channel and never share OTPs, PINs or passwords.
